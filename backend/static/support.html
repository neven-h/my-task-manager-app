Common questions

  • Sign-in trouble: check that you are using the email address connected to your account, then try resetting your password.
  • Calendar access: review or change calendar permission in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete your account: use the account deletion option in the app’s Settings screen. If you cannot access your account, contact support.
  • Financial information: market data and finance features are informational and are not financial advice.
