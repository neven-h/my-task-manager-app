Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

This policy explains how Dr Pitz Club collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address, username, authentication information, and security settings.
  • App content: tasks, notes, categories, clients, projects, attachments, and photos you choose to add.
  • Financial organization data: budgets, transactions, account labels, portfolio entries, and watchlists you choose to enter or import.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us for help.

Device permissions and related data

If you use calendar features, the app may request permission to create or manage calendar entries. You can change this permission in iPhone Settings. If you upload files or photos, the app accesses only the items you select.

How information is used

We use information to provide and secure the app, save and synchronize your content, operate requested features, respond to support requests, diagnose problems, and improve reliability.

Sharing and service providers

We may use hosting, storage, authentication, market-data, and infrastructure providers to operate the app. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information, use it for third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the app. Items placed in an in-app trash area may remain there temporarily before permanent deletion. You can request account deletion from the app’s Settings screen or by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices

You can update information in the app, change device permissions in iPhone Settings, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Children

Dr Pitz Club is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is posted.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email drpitz.club@gmail.com. For general help, visit our Support page.
